About SY Green Energy

Your Trusted Partner in Green Energy Solutions

SY Green Energy, established in 2022, is the leading manufacturer and project developer for an exclusive range of domestic and commercial biogas plants, CBG plant, green hydrogen plant, VPSA plant, bio-CNG plant, oxygen generation plants, nitrogen generation plants, solar plants, waste-to-energy solutions, and organic fertilizers. Our company was founded on the principle of delivering high-quality, "Nature Friendly" solutions that benefit our clients and mother nature.

We have skilled engineering professionals who provide infrastructural facilities to undertake all domestic, industrial, and commercial projects related to renewable energy. We render consultancy for project preparation, feasibility studies, site visits, project implementation, and awareness programs on waste management—a one-stop solution for all waste-to-energy projects.

End‑to‑end execution

From feasibility studies and detailed project reports to commissioning and training, we handle the complete project lifecycle with precision and care.

Waste‑to‑energy expertise

Strong expertise in converting organic waste streams into reliable green fuel and valuable organic fertilizers, reducing environmental impact.

Specialized engineering team

Skilled mechanical and process engineers with hands‑on experience across CBG, bio‑gas and industrial gas plants, ensuring optimal performance.

One‑stop solutions

Logo: Save the logo image from sygreenenergy.com (right-click the logo on the site and "Save image as") to this folder as logo.png. The header and footer will display it automatically. If logo.png is missing, the text "SY Green Energy" is shown instead.

Company at a Glance (CBG plant image): Add your CBG/bio-gas plant photo to this folder as cbg-plant-1.jpg. It will fill the area below the stats in the "Company at a Glance" section on the homepage. If the file is missing, a placeholder image is shown.
